SEO Audit and 6-Month Strategy

whitecardwebinars.com.au · Prepared by Pooya Hayati · November 2026

Summary

The site targets about 35,000 searches a month across the 32 terms I researched, and that demand is flat year over year. Almost all of it sits in state-level and city-level terms ("white card nsw", "white card qld", "white card sydney"), not suburb-level terms. The main problems are duplicate and overlapping pages, crawl waste, slow mobile speed, on-page gaps and missing Course schema. Fixing these and giving each state one strong page is the fastest route to better rankings.

Key findings

High priority · Duplicate and near-duplicate pages

  • Three pages exist as live duplicates with a -2 version: the step-by-step guide, the Sydney course page and the NSW review page. The -2 copies receive more internal links than the originals.
  • 42 suburb pages share one template: about 3,200 words each, about 92% similar meta descriptions, and only 1 to 2 internal links each.
  • Search demand for "white card online [suburb]" is minimal (10 to 30 searches a month for the variants checked).
  • Sydney is targeted by four overlapping pages.

High priority · Crawl waste

  • 134 internal URLs return a 301 redirect, including 58 old blog posts that all redirect to one page, and internal links that point to URLs without the trailing slash.
  • 60 CSS and JavaScript files are blocked by robots.txt, which can stop Google from rendering pages fully.

Medium · On-page issues

  • 24 page titles are over 60 characters, and the homepage title is 86.
  • 20 meta descriptions are too long, and 1 is missing.
  • State pages (NSW, QLD, ACT) use the same title and meta description pattern.
  • 6 duplicate H1s and 33 images with missing alt text.

Medium · Structured data

  • Present: Organization, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, EducationalOrganization.
  • Missing: Course schema. Service pages are typed as Article.
  • Review and rating markup is attached to the organization itself. Google does not display self-serving reviews, so it should be removed or replaced with genuine, visible reviews.

Strategy: months 1 to 6

Month 1: fix the foundations

  • Remove the robots.txt rules that block CSS and JavaScript files.
  • 301-redirect the -2 duplicates to the preferred pages, retire or redirect the 58 old posts, and update internal links to the correct URLs (with the trailing slash).
  • Fix the highest-impact speed issues: defer non-essential scripts, enable caching, convert images to WebP. Focus first on the NSW page's INP.
  • Set up a baseline report: rankings for the 32 terms, indexed pages, clicks and impressions.

Months 2 to 3: restructure content

  • Reduce the 42 suburb pages to the 8 to 10 with real demand and unique local content. Noindex or redirect the rest.
  • Give each state one strong page with a unique title, H1 and meta description, built around "white card [state]". Merge the four overlapping Sydney pages into one.
  • Add Course schema to service pages and fix the review markup.
  • Fix titles, meta descriptions, duplicate H1s and image alt text.
  • Publish a "white card replacement" page to capture that low-competition demand.

Months 4 to 6: grow

  • Content: FAQ and guide content for the questions learners ask (cost, same-day, online vs in-person by state, replacement), each page with a clear purpose and internal links to the state pages.
  • Backlinks: target industry bodies, construction and safety associations, training directories and relevant local sites, plus unlinked brand mentions.
  • Google Business Profile: request reviews from Queensland learners, link each profile to its state page, correct the inconsistent hours, and verify categories, services and photos.
  • AI search visibility: make answers short and clear on the pages, keep schema complete, and check monthly whether the brand appears in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews for "white card online" queries.

Reporting

A short report every two weeks: what changed, what moved in rankings, clicks and impressions, and what is next. Targets are agreed after the baseline; I don't promise rankings.

  • Review gap: Queensland has 176 reviews against 4,354 for Sydney, even though "white card qld" and "white card online qld" together are about 7,800 searches a month. A simple post-course review request for QLD learners would close this.

Optimization actions beyond data fixes

  • Review system: send every learner a short review link right after the course (email or SMS, with a QR code on the certificate page), reply to every review, and track review volume per month for each profile.
  • Services and courses: list each offer as its own service with a clear description (white card online NSW, QLD, same-day, replacement), and add prices and a booking link where the profile allows it.
  • Weekly posts: short posts on session times, same-day availability and common questions, each linking to the matching state page with a tracking tag, so profile clicks are measurable.
  • Q&A and description: seed the most common learner questions with accurate answers, and write the business description around real search terms ("white card online [state]") without stuffing.
  • Photos and video: add real photos and short clips (trainers, session screenshots) and refresh them regularly.
  • Guideline check: confirm the address setup follows Google's rules for a mostly online business, since a mismatch can lead to suspension. Monitor the profiles for edits or suspensions.
  • Measure and benchmark: track calls, website clicks and bookings from each profile, and check map-pack rankings for "white card online [city]" against the top three competitors, including their categories and review counts.
  • Site connection: add LocalBusiness or Organization schema with sameAs links to each profile, and embed the Google Maps and visible reviews on the state pages.

Case studies

didarhome.com (ecommerce)

  • Problem: filter and sort URLs created thousands of duplicates, pagination was broken, pages competed for the same keywords and the schema had errors.
  • Action: canonicals, noindex and robots rules, merged or redirected competing pages, one URL per key keyword, corrected schema.
  • Result: organic clicks grew from about 700 to 3,500+ a month in 12 months. One optimized page (carpet pricing) moved from page 2 to an average position of 1 for its keyword, and monthly clicks to it grew from about 25 to about 430.

saeeclinic.com (clinic)

  • Result: organic clicks grew from about 14K to 60K a month. On Google Ads, cost per lead fell from about $8 to $4 while monthly leads doubled.

Telina (cloud phone and CRM provider)

  • Handled SEO and GEO. ChatGPT now sends them about 2 to 3 leads a day.
